SREE SAI CUTTING TOOLS

Specialized cutting-tool manufacturing from Hyderabad.

Sree Sai Cutting Tools Pvt Ltd. manufactures precision cutting tools for automotive and engineering applications, with a focus on made-to-order tooling where geometry, material, finish and repeatability matter.

Our product focus includes gear hobs, spline cutters, chain and sprocket hobs, ratchet cutters, rack milling cutters, form milling cutters and special cutting tools manufactured to customer requirements.

We support requirements based on component drawings, existing tool drawings and customer-defined technical specifications. Our supplied requirements span multiple tool-steel grades, including M2, M35 / EM35, M42, M50 and selected powder-metallurgy HSS grades such as ASP30 and ASP2030, together with coated-tool requirements.
